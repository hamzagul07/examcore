## What WMA11 is testing

Expect core Pure 1 territory: algebra and functions, coordinate geometry, introductory calculus, sequences/series where specified, trigonometry foundations, and exam technique under timed conditions. Exact topic lists live in the official Pearson specification — use that as the checklist, not a Cambridge scheme of work photocopy.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Method visible** — examiners award M marks for a valid route even when arithmetic slips later.  
- **Equivalent forms** — simplified answers that match the scheme’s accepted equivalents.  
- **Dependent accuracy** — an early algebra error can wipe later A marks; rebuild from the mistake instead of carrying fantasy numbers.  
- **Notation discipline** — dy/dx, exact values, and stated domains when the question demands them.

## Revision plan that matches a modular unit

1. **Spec map (1 sitting)** — tick every WMA11 topic; star anything you have only seen in Cambridge notes.  
2. **Technique blocks (daily)** — 25–40 minutes on one skill (e.g. differentiation from first principles / polynomial factorisation), then one mixed exam-style question.  
3. **Full papers (weekly)** — sit under time; mark with the official scheme language, not “looks right.”  
4. **Second pass** — photograph or type the worst question into [MarkScheme WMA11 marking](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WMA11) so method/accuracy feedback is board-native.  
5. **UMS awareness** — after you have a marked raw total, read [IAL UMS / grade boundaries](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026) so you know how the unit feeds cash-in. Do not convert with a Cambridge calculator.

## Common WMA11 mistakes

| Mistake | Why it costs marks | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Skipping method lines | No M marks even if the final number is lucky | Write the operation that earns the mark |
| Mixing Cambridge command habits | Wrong depth for “show that” / “hence” | Practise Edexcel wording on real WMA11 papers |
| Exact value → early decimal | Accuracy mark lost; follow-through chaos | Keep exact form until the question asks to approximate |
| Ignoring the printed answer format | Scheme expects a specific form | Check the question’s “give your answer as…” line first |
| Treating UMS as raw % | Misreads progress | Raw first, UMS second — see the [UMS guide](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026) |

## FAQ

### Is WMA11 the same as AS Pure?

It is the IAL Pure 1 unit in Pearson’s modular structure. Naming differs by centre materials; trust the **unit code WMA11** on your entry, not informal “AS Pure” labels.

### Can I sit WMA11 in January and cash in later?

That is the modular point of IAL — units across sessions, then cash-in. Confirm your centre’s entry plan and retake rules early.

### Should I practise 9709 papers for WMA11?

Only for shared pure techniques. For exam timing and mark wording, use **WMA11** papers. Cross-board practice without a translation step trains the wrong reflexes.

### Does MarkScheme mark Mechanics/Statistics too?

Wave 1 IAL Maths marking includes WMA, WME, and WST unit codes on the mark picker. Start with the unit you will sit next.
