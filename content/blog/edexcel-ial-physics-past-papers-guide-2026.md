Edexcel International A Level **Physics** is modular. Practise by **unit** (WPH11–WPH16), not by pretending every paper is Cambridge 9702. This guide is the practice loop — we do not host Pearson PDFs.

## Quick answer

| Step | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Pick the unit you will sit next ([Physics hub](/edexcel/international-a-level/physics)) |
| 2 | Sit a past paper under time from Pearson / your centre |
| 3 | Self-mark with the official scheme (M/A language) |
| 4 | Second-pass the worst question on [MarkScheme](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WPH11) |
| 5 | Track UMS / cash-in — not Cambridge thresholds |

## Why not use Cambridge papers as a proxy?

9702 papers are excellent Physics practice — they are **not** the same mark dialect or modular cash-in. If you sit Edexcel, mark Edexcel. If you sit Cambridge, use Cambridge. MarkScheme keeps the board picker honest.

## After you mark

- Close one weak topic, then sit another unit paper.  
- Read [UMS / boundaries](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026) so raw totals map to cash-in goals.  
- Chemistry students: [IAL Chemistry marking](/blog/edexcel-ial-chemistry-marking-guide-2026).
