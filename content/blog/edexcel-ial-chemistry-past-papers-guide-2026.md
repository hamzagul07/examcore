Edexcel **International A Level Chemistry** is modular. Past papers are organised by **unit** (WCH11–WCH16), not by a single linear “Paper 1 / Paper 2” Cambridge rhythm.

## Quick answer

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Where do I start? | Your next cash-in unit — usually [WCH11](/edexcel/international-a-level/chemistry/wch11) |
| How do I mark? | Pearson M/A/B dialect on [/mark?board=edexcel](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WCH11) |
| UMS? | Same modular cash-in idea as IAL Maths — unit raw → UMS |
| Cambridge notes? | Useful for overlap; wrong for paper timing and scheme wording |

## The loop

1. Sit one unit paper under time.  
2. Mark with the official scheme language (method before polish).  
3. Re-mark the weakest question on [MarkScheme](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WCH11).  
4. Fix one technique block, then rotate units.
