**OxfordAQA International A-level Mathematics** is a **linear** qualification (papers, not Edexcel-style modular WMA cash-in). Content overlaps Cambridge 9709 Pure / Mechanics / Statistics ideas, but paper rhythm and scheme wording differ.

## Practise loop

1. Sit a full paper under time.  
2. Mark with point/method language the scheme expects.  
3. Second-pass weak questions on MarkScheme with **OxfordAQA** selected.  
4. Use overlapping [Cambridge 9709 courses](/courses/9709) for idea drills — then return to OxfordAQA papers.
