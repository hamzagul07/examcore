## What WCH11 rewards

Expect early physical and organic skills: electron configurations that justify bonding, balanced equations with state symbols, and structured “show that” calculation routes.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Method visible** — M marks for a valid route even when arithmetic slips later.  
- **Units and significant figures** — leave answers in the form the question asks for.  
- **Terminology** — use the scheme’s language for bonding and structure, not vague paraphrases.  
- **Dependent accuracy** — rebuild from an early slip instead of inventing a final answer.

## Revision plan

1. **Spec map** — tick every WCH11 topic; star anything you have only seen in Cambridge notes.  
2. **Technique blocks** — 25–40 minutes on one skill, then one mixed exam-style question.  
3. **Full papers** — sit under time; mark with Pearson scheme language.  
4. **Second pass** — photograph or type the worst question into [MarkScheme WCH11 marking](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WCH11).
