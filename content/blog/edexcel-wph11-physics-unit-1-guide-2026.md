## What WPH11 rewards

Expect core early Physics skills: clear diagrams, defined quantities, substitution with units, and structured “show that” working. Exact topic lists live in the official Pearson specification — use that as the checklist, not a Cambridge scheme of work photocopy.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Method visible** — examiners award M marks for a valid route even when arithmetic slips later.  
- **Units and SF** — leave answers in the form the question asks for; do not invent precision.  
- **Dependent accuracy** — an early slip can wipe later A marks; rebuild from the mistake.  
- **Diagrams that earn** — labelled forces, rays, or circuit symbols when the scheme expects them.

## Revision plan for a modular Physics unit

1. **Spec map** — tick every WPH11 topic; star anything you have only seen in Cambridge notes.  
2. **Technique blocks** — 25–40 minutes on one skill (e.g. SUVAT with vectors, standing waves), then one mixed exam-style question.  
3. **Full papers** — sit under time; mark with Pearson scheme language, not “looks right.”  
4. **Second pass** — photograph or type the worst question into [MarkScheme WPH11 marking](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WPH11).  
5. **UMS awareness** — after a marked raw total, read [IAL UMS / grade boundaries](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026) so you know how the unit feeds cash-in. Do not convert with a Cambridge calculator.

## Common WPH11 mistakes

| Mistake | Why it costs marks | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Skipping method lines | No M marks even if the final number is lucky | Write the operation that earns the mark |
| Wrong or missing units | Accuracy dropped even when algebra is fine | State unit with every physical answer |
| Mixing Cambridge command habits | Wrong depth for “show that” / “hence” | Practise Edexcel wording on real WPH papers |
