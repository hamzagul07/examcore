**WMA12** is Edexcel International A Level **Pure Mathematics 2** — the modular Pure unit that deepens algebra, logs/exponentials, trigonometry and calculus after WMA11. It is not Cambridge 9709 Paper 2 with a different code. Content overlaps Pure 2 idea families, but Pearson’s **paper rhythm and mark scheme dialect** still decide the marks.

## What WMA12 is testing

Expect Pure 2 territory: deeper algebra and functions, logarithmic and exponential equations, harder trig identities and equations, and differentiation/integration that builds on Pure 1 technique. Exact topic lists live in the official Pearson specification — use that as the checklist.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Method still visible** — M marks for a valid route even when arithmetic slips later.  
- **Exact forms** — logs, exponentials and trig answers often lose A marks when rounded early.  
- **Dependent accuracy** — an early algebra error can wipe a chain of A marks; rebuild from the mistake.  
- **“Show that” discipline** — do not reverse-engineer from the printed result without earning the method.

## Revision plan that matches a modular unit

1. **Spec map (1 sitting)** — tick every WMA12 topic; star anything you only saw in Cambridge Pure 2 notes.  
2. **Technique blocks (daily)** — 25–40 minutes on one skill (e.g. log equations, trig identities), then one mixed exam-style question.  
3. **Full papers (weekly)** — sit under time; mark with Pearson scheme language, not “looks right.”  
4. **Second pass** — photograph or type the worst question into [MarkScheme WMA12 marking](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WMA12).  
5. **UMS awareness** — after a marked raw total, read [IAL UMS / grade boundaries](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026). Do not convert with a Cambridge calculator.

## Common WMA12 mistakes

| Mistake | Why it costs marks | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Treating it like “harder WMA11” without new techniques | Misses log/trig identity method marks | Practise WMA12-only topic blocks weekly |
| Early decimals in exact answers | Accuracy mark lost; follow-through chaos | Keep exact form until the question asks to approximate |
| Skipping identity steps | No M marks even if the final line is lucky | Write the identity that earns the mark |
| Mixing Cambridge command habits | Wrong depth for “show that” / “hence” | Practise Edexcel wording on real WMA12 papers |
| Ignoring UMS cash-in | Misreads whether to retake Pure 2 vs Pure 1 | Raw first, UMS second — see the [UMS guide](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026) |

## FAQ

### Do I need WMA11 before WMA12?

Most centres sequence Pure 1 before Pure 2. Confirm your entry plan — modular cash-in still needs the units your school registers.

### Should I practise 9709 Pure 2 papers for WMA12?

Only for shared techniques. For exam timing and mark wording, use **WMA12** papers.
