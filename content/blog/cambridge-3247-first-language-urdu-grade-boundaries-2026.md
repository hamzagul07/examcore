## June 2025 thresholds (verified)

These are the most recent published thresholds, taken from the official Cambridge PDF. They are **not** the June 2026 numbers — nobody has those until Cambridge publishes them — but they are the right yardstick for a marked past paper.

| Component | Paper | Max | A | B | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Paper 1 | 50 | **43** | 39 | 36 | 27 | 18 |
| 02 | Paper 2 | 50 | **42** | 39 | 36 | 26 | 17 |

Overall, both papers, 100 after weighting: A\* 92, A 85, B 78, C 72, D 53, E 35.

Those aggregate boundaries sit unusually high — 85 of 100 for an A, 92 for an A\*. That is normal for a first-language paper and not a sign the grade is out of reach: when most candidates are fluent speakers, the marks bunch near the top and the cut-offs rise to separate them. The gap between C at 72 and D at 53 is where the real spread is.

## What these numbers are, and are not

Grade boundaries are the minimum **raw marks** for each grade on each component. They are set **after** every script is marked, so the same grade means the same standard each year: a harder paper moves the cut-off **down**, it does not put the grade out of reach.

Two things trip people up on results day:

1. **A\* is not a component grade.** First Language Urdu is two 50-mark papers weighted to 100. Your per-paper marks feed a weighted aggregate, and A\* is set on that aggregate only.
2. **Variants have different boundaries.** Match the component code on your statement of entry before comparing anything.

## How to estimate your grade

1. Sit a recent paper under timed conditions for your exact entry route.
2. Mark strictly, and log every lost mark before deciding an answer basically counts.
3. Compare each component's raw mark to the table above, or use the [3247 calculator](/tools/grade-boundary-calculator/3247).
4. Re-check on 18 August, once the June 2026 table exists.

## When the June 2026 numbers land

Cambridge releases O-Level grades on **18 August 2026 at 05:00 GMT** (06:00 BST, 10:00 PKT). Component threshold tables follow the results rather than arriving with them, and Cambridge does not pre-announce the exact hour. This page is updated from the official PDF as soon as there is one — no estimates, no rumours.
