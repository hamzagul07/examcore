## June 2025 thresholds (verified)

These are the most recent published thresholds, taken from the official Cambridge PDF. They are **not** the June 2026 numbers — nobody has those until Cambridge publishes them — but they are the right yardstick for a marked past paper.

| Component | Paper | Max | A | B | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Paper 1 | 50 | **28** | 24 | 20 | 18 | 15 |
| 12 | Paper 1 | 50 | **31** | 27 | 23 | 19 | 15 |
| 21 | Paper 2 | 50 | **28** | 23 | 19 | 15 | 12 |
| 22 | Paper 2 | 50 | **29** | 25 | 21 | 17 | 14 |

Islamiyat is entered on one of two option routes and they are **not** interchangeable. **AX** combines components 11 and 21; **AY** combines 12 and 22. Overall out of 100 after weighting — **AX**: A\* 65, A 56, B 47, C 39. **AY**: A\* 68, A 60, B 52, C 44.

AY sat three to five marks higher at every grade that session, so comparing your marks against the wrong option route is the fastest way to misread how you did. Check which option code is on your statement of entry first.

## What these numbers are, and are not

Grade boundaries are the minimum **raw marks** for each grade on each component. They are set **after** every script is marked, so the same grade means the same standard each year: a harder paper moves the cut-off **down**, it does not put the grade out of reach.

Two things trip people up on results day:

1. **A\* is not a component grade.** Islamiyat is two 50-mark papers weighted to 100. Your per-paper marks feed a weighted aggregate, and A\* is set on that aggregate only.
2. **Variants have different boundaries.** Match the component code on your statement of entry before comparing anything.

## How to estimate your grade

1. Sit a recent paper under timed conditions for your exact entry route.
2. Mark strictly, and log every lost mark before deciding an answer basically counts.
3. Compare each component's raw mark to the table above, or use the [2058 calculator](/tools/grade-boundary-calculator/2058).
4. Re-check on 18 August, once the June 2026 table exists.

## When the June 2026 numbers land

Cambridge releases O-Level grades on **18 August 2026 at 05:00 GMT** (06:00 BST, 10:00 PKT). Component threshold tables follow the results rather than arriving with them, and Cambridge does not pre-announce the exact hour.
