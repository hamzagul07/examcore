## What WPH12 rewards

Expect Unit 2 territory from the Pearson specification (materials, waves/electric applications and related “physics at work” contexts). Use the official spec as the checklist.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Method visible** — M marks for a valid route even when arithmetic slips.  
- **Units and SF** — leave answers in the form the question asks for.  
- **Dependent accuracy** — rebuild from early slips instead of carrying fantasy numbers.  
- **Context reading** — applied questions still need physics, not story padding.

## Revision plan

1. Spec tick for **WPH12 only**.  
2. Technique blocks (25–40 min) then one mixed exam question.  
3. Full papers under time; mark with Pearson scheme language.  
4. Second pass on [MarkScheme WPH12](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WPH12).
