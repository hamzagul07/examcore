## What WBI11 rewards

Expect early Biology skills: precise definitions, process sequences (e.g. transport), and answers that use the **scheme’s idea**, not a vague paraphrase. Exact topic lists live in the official Pearson specification — use that as the checklist, not a Cambridge scheme of work photocopy.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Definitions that match the scheme** — close synonyms can earn; waffle does not.  
- **Process order** — missing a step often costs an independent mark.  
- **Command-word depth** — “describe” vs “explain” vs “compare” change what earns.  
- **No invented biology** — do not add pathways the question never asked for.

## Revision plan for a modular Biology unit

1. **Spec map** — tick every WBI11 topic; star anything you have only seen in Cambridge 9700 notes.  
2. **Phrase blocks** — 20–30 minutes rewriting key definitions in scheme language, then one short exam question.  
3. **Full papers** — sit under time; mark with Pearson wording, not “sounds right.”  
4. **Second pass** — photograph or type the weakest answer into [MarkScheme WBI11 marking](/mark?board=edexcel&subject=WBI11).  
5. **UMS awareness** — raw first, then cash-in planning on the [Biology grade-boundaries hub](/edexcel/international-a-level/biology/grade-boundaries).
