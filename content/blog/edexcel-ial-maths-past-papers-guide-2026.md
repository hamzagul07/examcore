## A session that actually improves UMS

1. **Pick unit + series** — same unit you will sit; prefer a recent series for style, older ones for volume.  
2. **Timed sit** — full unit time; no phone, no peeking at MS mid-paper.  
3. **Strict self-mark** — method lines first, then accuracy; note follow-through.  
4. **Second pass** — photograph the messiest question into MarkScheme with `board=edexcel` and the unit code.  
5. **Log raw → think UMS** — only after a marked raw total; see the [UMS guide](/blog/edexcel-ial-maths-grade-boundaries-ums-2026).  
6. **One repair block** — the topic that lost the most M/A marks, not a random YouTube binge.

## What not to do

| Trap | Why it fails |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mixing 9709 papers as “close enough” | Wrong timing and mark wording |
| Marking only the final answer | Misses method marks that decide UMS |
| Chasing leaked “predicted” papers | Integrity risk; weak training signal |
| Converting with Cambridge calculators | Different currency — PUM ≠ IAL UMS |
| Sitting three units the week before cash-in | No time to repair method habits |

## FAQ

### How many past papers before a unit sitting?

Enough that your last two timed papers feel familiar in pacing and mark language — often 4–8 full units for a first cash-in, more if Mechanics/Statistics are new. Quality of marking beats raw count.

### Jan vs June vs Oct — which is “harder”?

Difficulty is absorbed into boundaries/UMS. Practise the series you will sit when you can; otherwise any recent series trains the same skills.

### Can I use UK Edexcel A-Level papers?

Overlap exists in Pure topics, but International unit codes and papers are the ones that match your entry. Prefer **IAL** papers for WMA/WME/WST.

### When will Physics/Chemistry papers be marked here?

Shells exist; marking expands after IAL Maths conversion clears the gates in [BOARD_EXPANSION_ENGINE](/edexcel). Maths is the prove-it dialect.
