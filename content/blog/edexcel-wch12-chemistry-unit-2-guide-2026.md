## What WCH12 rewards

Expect energetics, group trends, and early organic (halogenoalkanes/alcohols) from the Pearson specification. Use that checklist — not a Cambridge scheme photocopy.

What separates strong scripts:

- **Equations with state symbols** when the scheme expects them.  
- **Method then accuracy** — valid energy/organic routes still earn M marks.  
- **Mechanism / reagent discipline** — wrong reagent often loses the A mark even if the idea is close.  
- **Significant figures and units** on numerical energetics.

## Revision plan

1. Spec tick for **WCH12 only**.  
2. Technique blocks (energetics calculations; organic reagents) then one mixed question.  
3. Full papers under time; mark with Pearson scheme language.
